Elixir Lab

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

Elixir Lab is an educational app for preparing pharmacognosy and phytochemistry reagents, following test procedures and recording test results. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

Elixir Lab does not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in and no advertising, and it does not use cookies, device identifiers or fingerprinting.

Anonymous visit counts. To see how many people use the app, it counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. When you are online, it records which main section was opened (for example Tests, Atlas or Calculators). GoatCounter also records general technical details such as browser type, screen size and country. It never receives test results, notes, typed text, photos or anything you enter. GoatCounter does not store IP addresses and does not use cookies; see the GoatCounter privacy policy.

Two optional questions. On first launch the app may ask what you are (student, researcher, teacher or other) and your college type (pharmacy, science, agriculture, medicine or other). Both are optional and can be skipped. If you answer, only those two choices are counted, with no link to you.

Information stored on your device

Results sheets, leaf descriptions (including any leaf photos you add), notes and saved (starred) items that you create are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them permanently.

Internet use

When online, the app downloads its content (reagents and test procedures) from its web host, GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app works offline after the first launch.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your microphone, location or contacts. It uses the camera or photo gallery only when you choose to add a leaf photo, and that photo stays on your device.

Children

Elixir Lab is intended for university students, educators and laboratory professionals. It is not directed at children.

Safety disclaimer

Information in the app is for educational purposes. Always follow your institution's laboratory safety rules and verify procedures against current official references.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
